# Compare scholarly inquiries

For each consequential work, reconstruct the problem its author faced and how
the inspected material develops an answer. Keep statements, your interpretation,
and criticism distinguishable. Abstract-only access permits a limited summary,
not a claim to have resolved a contested passage.

An author's terms acquire force from the difficulty they address. Before grouping
works under a shared question, establish what is actually shared and what the
comparison changes. Conceptual resemblance does not establish historical influence;
chronology does not establish progress. Do not manufacture mutually exclusive
schools or parallel positions to complete a table.

For instance, a theory of how a work is identified and a description of how it
is experienced may intersect without offering rival answers to one question.
Explain why the experience account bears on identification before treating it
as a refutation. This example illustrates a comparison problem, not a position
attributed to any specific thinker.

When a central reading remains disputed, preserve the decisive passage, its role,
and what each plausible reading explains. Consult
[interpreting primary texts](../../../modules/close-reading.md) where necessary. A synthesis
should not settle a dispute merely by assigning one author to a convenient school.

Organize prose through the relationships the research establishes. A paragraph
may explain a disagreement's basis, trace a conceptual change, or defend a shared
reading; it need not follow a fixed problem-position-verdict structure. If the
material supports convergence, do not create a conflict to sound analytical.

End at the degree of understanding the requested synthesis permits. A reading
path can identify the next consequential passage; a substantive review should
explain what the comparison itself makes clearer. Neither needs an invented gap.
Carry unresolved material forward with the map instead of reducing everything
to finished stance summaries.
