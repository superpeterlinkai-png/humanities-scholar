# Short essay in aesthetics

Using only `source.md`, write a 900–1200-word essay in English that develops your own account of what a visitor can aesthetically appreciate in The Rehearsal Room.

Choose a substantive question raised by the materials and pursue it through a connected argument. Give the theoretical fragments and particular encounters enough analysis to show what changes when you bring them together. You may agree with, revise, or reject R, S, or the artist. Do not treat the recorded reactions as a vote that determines the correct interpretation.

At some point, address what the mistaken-sign afternoon changes about your account. Consider the venue's proposal where it bears on your argument; you need not produce a design recommendation. End with the understanding your analysis has established and what follows from it.

All source materials are fictional constructions. Do not attribute them to real scholars, import additional artworks, or fabricate citations. Use stable labels such as “Visit D” when identifying evidence. There is no required paragraph count or prescribed argumentative template. Deliver the finished essay only.
