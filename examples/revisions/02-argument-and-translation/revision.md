# 阅读经验、作品同一性与解释

## 中文修订段落

同一部小说可能引发不同的阅读经验：同一段叙述可以使一位读者感到疏离，也可以使另一位读者感到亲近。两位读者的感受有别，同一部作品仍然可以是两种经验的共同对象。要由感受不同推到作品不同，就须把经验方式的差异当作区分作品的依据，而这个设想本身并未给出两者之间的联系。因此，阅读经验的差异不能单独决定作品的同一性。感受与解释之间还有一项区别：“我感到疏离”陈述的是读者的经验，关于叙述怎样在某次阅读中造成疏离感的说法则对作品提出了一个解释。后者要说明叙述中哪些措辞或关系支持这一理解；另一位读者感到亲近，本身既不是对这个解释的反驳，也不是不同解释同样有效的证明。感受差异为解释提出了不同的说明任务，解释之间的比较则考察各自怎样说明这段叙述。承认读者的感受不同，与比较解释所给出的理由，由此可以同时成立。

## English translation

The same novel may give rise to different reading experiences: one passage may leave one reader feeling estranged and another feeling a sense of closeness. The readers' feelings differ, yet the same work can remain the common object of both experiences. To move from different feelings to different works, one would have to treat a difference in the way a work is experienced as a basis for distinguishing works, and the hypothetical example itself supplies no connection between the two. Differences in reading experience therefore cannot by themselves settle the identity of the work. There is a further distinction between feeling and interpretation: “I feel estranged” reports the reader's experience, whereas an account of how the narrative gives rise to estrangement in a particular reading offers an interpretation of the work. Such an account has to explain which words or relations in the narrative support it; another reader's sense of closeness does not by itself refute that interpretation or establish that different interpretations are equally valid. Different feelings present interpretation with different things to explain, while a comparison of interpretations examines how each accounts for the passage. Acknowledging differences in readers' feelings is thus compatible with comparing the reasons offered by their interpretations.

## 修订与译法说明

这次实质修订将原稿关于评价标准的提醒展开成感受陈述与解释主张的比较，继续采用第二轮选定的两个否定结论。英文以本次中文为准：`the identity of the work` 对应跨阅读的作品同一性，`reading experience` 对应阅读经验，`equally valid` 对应解释效力的比较；`may`、`can` 及 `by itself` 保留例子的可能性和论证的范围。
